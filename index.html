🔗 WHAT ARE YOU ACTUALLY DOING?

In Bitcoin, miners compete to add new blocks to the blockchain by finding a special number called a nonce. This nonce, when combined with the block's data and hashed, produces a valid result — but there's no shortcut to find it. Miners try billions of values until they get lucky. In this game, your math answer IS the nonce — solve the puzzle, find the nonce, mine the block, and secure the chain! ⛓️
